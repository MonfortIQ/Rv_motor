Our Heritage & Expertise

Dedicated to the Freedom of the Open Highway

Founded by seasoned RV overlanders and RVIA Master Certified technicians, RoadNest was built on a simple conviction: motorhome care demands its own specialized engineering discipline.

The RoadNest Story

Craftsmanship Born on the American Road

In 2014, after spending years watching fellow travelers get turned away from automotive shops or stranded for weeks waiting for dealership parts, founder Arthur Henderson launched RoadNest in Denver, Colorado.

Unlike traditional garages that view an RV simply as an inconveniently large truck, our facility was engineered specifically around the dual nature of recreational vehicles: half heavy automotive transport, half luxury self-contained living habitat.

Our Mission

To eliminate breakdown anxiety through rigorous, proactive RV maintenance and transparent, honest diagnostics.

Our Vision

To build North America’s most dependable, traveler-first RV care ecosystem with digital records accessible anywhere.

Our Foundations

Values That Guide Every Turn of the Wrench

Traveler Safety First

We never compromise on brake torque, tire integrity, propane pressure, or structural slide locks.

Total Transparency

Detailed digital photos and clear itemized estimates for every repair line item before any work begins.

OEM Standards

We strictly adhere to manufacturer service bulletins for Lippert, Dometic, Aqua-Hot, and Cummins Onan.

Adventure Passion

Our team camps, overlands, and tows. We know the exact stresses your rig endures on backcountry passes.

A Decade of Growth

The RoadNest Journey

2014

RoadNest Founded in Denver

Opened our first 2-bay dedicated RV workshop in Denver, focusing initially on rooftop leak sealing and winterization.

2017

Expanded 50-Foot Commercial Facility

Constructed eight 50-foot pull-through bays equipped with high-clearance rotary hoists capable of servicing Class A diesel pushers.

2020

Advanced Electronics & Solar Integration

Introduced specialized lithium LiFePO4 battery installation, pure sine wave inverter diagnostics, and digital multiplex system tuning.

2023

Mobile RV Emergency Fleet Launched

Deployed fully outfitted mobile workshop vans providing on-site campground repairs and emergency roadside support across Colorado.

2026

Digital Owner Maintenance Portal

Launched our comprehensive digital cloud portal, empowering over 4,500 RV owners with instant maintenance records and warranty tracking.
