RV Plumbing Leak Detection: Finding Hidden PEX Drops and Tank Issues

Carlos Morales (HVAC & Plumbing Lead) June 10, 2026 5 min read

Nothing ruins a peaceful evening at camp quite like the sound of your 12V water pump cycling for 2 seconds every twenty minutes when no faucets are open.

That intermittent cycling is your coach's pressure sensor signaling a micro-leak. Learn how to isolate city water check valves, toilet foot valves, and water heater pressure relief drains.
