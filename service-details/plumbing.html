RV Plumbing & Holding Tank Repair

From fresh water PEX leak repairs and silent 12V pumps to stubborn waste valve replacements, RoadNest keeps your coach sanitary, leak-free, and road-ready.

Book This Service

Why Specialized Service Matters

Unlike residential plumbing that stays stationary, an RV's PEX lines, compression fittings, and tank gate valves endure constant vibration, freeze-thaw cycles, and shifting water weight. Undetected micro-leaks behind cabinetry quickly cause subfloor swelling, mold, and costly damage.

Warning Signs & Common Problems

Water Pump Cycling with Faucets Closed

Indicates a pinhole pressure leak somewhere in the internal plumbing or a failing pump check valve.

Persistent Waste Odors Inside Coach

Failed black tank roof vent cap, dried P-traps, or broken vacuum breaker beneath the bathroom sink.

Stuck or Leaking Black/Grey Gate Valves

Dried rubber seals or cable binding making tank dumping messy and unreliable at dump stations.

Failing Tank Monitor Level Sensors

Struvite buildup and paper debris coating sensor probes causing false 100% full readings.

Included Technical Work

  • 50 PSI Hydrostatic Pressure Leak Test: Pressurizing city water lines with digital gauge to confirm zero internal drop over 30 minutes.
  • Shurflo & Flojet 12V Pump Diagnostic: Measuring pump flow GPM, pressure switch cutoff, and intake strainer sediment cleaning.
  • Black & Grey Waste Valve Replacement: Installing heavy-duty Valterra cable-actuated gate valves with new silicone seal packs.
  • Tank Enzymatic Hydro-Flushing: High-pressure rotating nozzle cleaning of internal black tank sensors to restore accurate readings.
