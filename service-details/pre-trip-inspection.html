65-Point Road Readiness

Comprehensive RV Pre-Trip Safety Inspection

Before you set off on cross-country road trips or seasonal migrations, ensure every critical system is verified by RVIA certified master inspectors.

Book This Service

Why Specialized Service Matters

A minor mechanical oversight—such as low tire pressure, dry-rotted brake magnets, or a hairline roof crack—can escalate into an expensive multi-thousand-dollar emergency far from home. Our 65-point inspection delivers complete peace of mind with a full digital report.

Warning Signs & Common Problems

Approaching Multi-State Summer Tour

Long highway mileage and extreme altitude shifts require verification of all baseline systems.

Recently Purchased Used RV

Uncover hidden water leaks, outdated tires, and electrical modifications before your maiden voyage.

RV Stored for More Than 3 Months

Fluid condensation, dead battery cells, rodent wire damage, and stuck valves must be checked.

Included Technical Work

  • Chassis & Running Gear Safety Check: Tire cold PSI, tread depth, date codes, lug nut torque, brake linings, and suspension shackles.
  • Propane LP System Pressure Drop Test: Manometer testing of propane regulator and lines to verify zero combustible gas leaks.
  • Rooftop Seam & Seal Penetration Audit: Hands-on examination of all Dicor lap joints, skylight domes, and AC gaskets.
  • Full Electrical & Appliance Operational Test: Testing microwave, water heater, rooftop AC, furnace, refrigerator, and safety alarms.
