Why Preventive Maintenance Matters for Motorhomes

Unlike passenger cars that weigh 3,500 lbs, a Class A diesel pusher or Class C motorhome weighs between 12,000 and 45,000 lbs. That immense load exerts relentless stress on transmissions, cooling systems, brake linings, and suspension joints—especially when climbing Rocky Mountain passes or braking down steep declines.

Our certified technicians follow chassis-specific service intervals for Freightliner, Spartan, Ford F-53, Mercedes-Benz Sprinter, and Chevy Workhorse chassis.

Warning Signs Your RV Needs Chassis Attention

Spongy or Pulling Brakes

Indicates worn brake drums, air brake pressure drop, or overheating hydraulic calipers.

Elevated Transmission Temps

Fluid thermal breakdown that can lead to catastrophic transmission slip on highway grades.

Steering Play or Wandering

Dry tie-rod ends, worn kingpins, or loose steering stabilizer shocks causing fatigue.

Exhaust Smoke or Hesitation

Clogged fuel water separators, dirty diesel particulate filters (DPF), or fouled injectors.

What's Included in RoadNest Preventive Care

  • Engine Oil & Heavy Duty Filter Flush: Full synthetic diesel/gas formulation matched to manufacturer viscosity specifications.
  • Chassis Grease & Zerk Fitting Lube: Complete lubrication of all front suspension kingpins, drag links, and universal joints.
