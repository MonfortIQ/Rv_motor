RV Roof Resealing & Membrane Repair

Protect your motorhome from roof leaks, structural wood rot, and water intrusion. We restore Dicor lap seams, vent collars, skylights, and TPO membranes.

Book This Service

Why Specialized Service Matters

The roof is your RV's most vulnerable defense against the elements. UV sunlight, low-hanging tree branches, and highway vibrations cause factory lap sealant to dry, crack, and shrink. Water penetration can cause catastrophic structural rot before an interior leak is ever spotted.

Warning Signs & Common Problems

Cracked or Peeling Lap Sealant

Visible voids, shrinkage cracks, or bubbling around roof vents, plumbing stacks, or front caps.

Brown Stains Along Interior Ceiling Seams

The hallmark warning sign that water has penetrated the roof deck and saturated ceiling insulation.

Spongy or Soft Spots Underfoot

Indicates that moisture has compromised the plywood or OSB roof decking, requiring membrane removal.

Tree Branch Tears or Punctures

Gashes in the TPO or EPDM rubber membrane exposing raw underlayment to rainwater.

Included Technical Work

  • Aged Sealant Mechanical Scraping: Carefully removing loose, chalky lap sealant down to a clean substrate without gouging membrane.
  • Denatured Alcohol Solvent Cleaning: Degreasing the joint area so fresh self-leveling sealant forms a permanent chemical bond.
  • Dicor Self-Leveling Sealant Application: Generously sealing front/rear end caps, plumbing vents, refrigerator stacks, and skylight flanges.
  • Eternabond MicroSealant Seam Reinforcement: Applying 4-inch UV-stabilized tape across high-stress transition seams for decades of leak protection.
