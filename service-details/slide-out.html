RV Slide-Out Calibration & Mechanism Repair

Smooth, jam-free room extension every time you make camp. We service Lippert In-Wall (Schwintek), through-frame hydraulic, cable, and rack-and-pinion rooms.

Book This Service

Why Specialized Service Matters

Modern RV slide-outs add hundreds of square feet of luxury living space, but they rely on millimeter-precise alignment, electric motor synchronization, and heavy hydraulic rams. A binding slide can bend gear tracks, burn out motors, or tear exterior wiper seals.

Warning Signs & Common Problems

Slide Room Extends Crooked or Binds

One motor running faster than the other, causing the room to cock sideways in the wall cutout.

Loud Popping or Grinding Gear Noise

Stripped spur gear teeth, damaged rack tracks, or broken shoe gibs inside the aluminum column.

Water Leaking Inside When Slide Is Extended

Torn or inverted rubber wiper bulb seals allowing wind-driven rain into the sidewall.

Hydraulic Fluid Puddles or Creeping Rooms

Internal bypass leak in hydraulic actuator cylinders causing the slide room to creep open while driving.

Included Technical Work

  • Dual Motor Hall-Effect Resynchronization: Electronic recalibration of motor rotation pulses to ensure perfectly parallel room movement.
  • Gear Rack & Column Bearing Clearance Check: Measuring top-to-bottom column pitch and replacing worn plastic slide wear pads.
  • Hydraulic Ram Cylinder Seal & Hose Inspection: Testing hydraulic relief valve pressures and inspecting crimped hoses for weeping.
  • Automotive Rubber Wiper Seal Conditioning: Applying UV seal conditioners to restore elasticity and prevent tearing.
