Running Gear Safety

RV Tire Inspection & Wheel Bearing Repack

Prevent dangerous highway blowouts and wheel bearing seizures. Comprehensive running gear maintenance for fifth wheels, travel trailers, and motorhomes.

Book This Service

Why Specialized Service Matters

RV tires endure extreme continuous static loads and extended exposure to sunlight UV degradation. Unlike auto tires that wear out their tread, RV tires typically fail from internal carcass dry rot and aging. Wheel bearings carry up to 7,000 lbs per axle and must be repacked annually.

Warning Signs & Common Problems

Hub Running Scorching Hot to the Touch

Lack of high-temperature grease or overtightened spindle nut causing bearing breakdown.

Sidewall Weather Checking or Cracking

UV ozone breakdown that compromises tire sidewall strength, dramatically increasing blowout risk.

Uneven Tread Wear or Scalloping

Bent trailer axle, loose wheel bearings, or out-of-balance heavy tire assemblies.

Weak or Pulling Electric Trailer Brakes

Grease leaking through rear seal onto brake shoes or worn electromagnets.

Included Technical Work

  • Bearing Degrease, Inspection & Hand Repack: Cleaning inner/outer roller bearings, inspecting races for pitting, and repacking with Lucas Red 'N' Tacky grease.
  • Double-Lip Grease Seal Replacement: Installing fresh spring-loaded neoprene grease seals to prevent grease from contaminating brake shoes.
  • Electric Brake Shoe & Magnet Adjustment: Burnishing brake drum surfaces, adjusting star wheels, and measuring magnet resistance in ohms.
  • Tire DOT Date Code & Tread Micrometer Audit: Checking tire age against the 5-year replacement threshold and verifying cold PSI.

Frequently Asked Questions

Trailer manufacturers and axle builders (Dexter, Lippert) mandate repacking wheel bearings every 12,000 miles or 12 months—whichever comes first.

Regardless of remaining tread depth, RV industry safety standards mandate replacing tires at 5 to 6 years of age from their DOT manufacture date stamp due to internal rubber degradation.
