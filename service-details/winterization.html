RV Winterization & Freeze Guard

Protect your water lines, holding tanks, toilet valves, water heater, and batteries from catastrophic freeze damage before sub-zero winter temperatures arrive.

Book Winterization ($169)

Why Professional Winterization Is Critical

Water expands by 9% as it freezes, producing up to 30,000 PSI of internal hydraulic pressure. In an RV, that pressure effortlessly splits brass fittings, shatters PEX piping behind internal walls, cracks toilet foot valves, and bursts 10-gallon water heater tanks.

A single cracked line can flood your RV subfloor during spring thaw, resulting in thousands of dollars in hidden structural rot and toxic mold. Our guaranteed winterization service protects your investment down to -50°F.

Warning Signs of Winter Freeze Risks

Uninsulated Low-Point Drains

Trapped residual water in plastic drain caps will freeze and crack the entire manifold.

Toilet Foot Pedal Leakage

The thin water valve inside Thetford and Dometic porcelain toilets is the #1 freeze casualty.

Our 7-Step Guaranteed Winterization Protocol

  • Water Heater Bypass & Drain: Anode rod removal, tank sediment flush, and bypass valve engagement to safeguard water heater coils.
  • Compressed Air Line Evacuation: Regulated 30 PSI air blowout through city water inlet to clear residual moisture from low spots.
  • Propylene Glycol Pink Antifreeze Induction: Pumping non-toxic -50°F rated antifreeze through hot and cold PEX circuits until deep pink flows at every tap.
  • Holding Tank Drain & Gate Valve Lube: Complete drainage of fresh, black, and grey tanks with valve seal silicone conditioning.
  • P-Trap & Macerator Fill: Pouring antifreeze into kitchen sinks, shower traps, and washing machine drain lines.

Frequently Asked Questions

We use only virgin propylene glycol RV-rated antifreeze that is 100% non-toxic, safe for potable water systems, and harmless to rubber seals. We never use automotive ethylene glycol.

Residential refrigerators require specialized solenoid cycling to winterize ice maker lines and water dispenser filters. Our certified technicians service Samsung, Whirlpool, and LG RV residential units.
